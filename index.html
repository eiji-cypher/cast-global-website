Our story

From corporate logistics to serving my community

My name is Paul Aliman, founder of CAST Global Shipping & Freight.

After many years in the transportation and logistics industry, I saw firsthand how customers often felt like just another number. I believed there was a better way: professional logistics services, with every customer treated like family.

What started as a vision to help individuals and small businesses with their shipping needs grew into CAST Global Shipping & Freight, a company dedicated to personalized service and reliable logistics solutions. Today we serve customers throughout Orange County, California and beyond with domestic and international shipping and freight services.

As a small business owner, I understand the challenges that families, entrepreneurs and growing businesses face. Our mission is simple: to provide dependable logistics solutions with integrity, transparency, efficiency and a personal touch.

Whether you're shipping a single package, moving freight across the country, managing inventory, or sending balikbayan boxes to your loved ones in the Philippines, we're here to help every step of the way. Thank you for trusting us.

Paul Aliman
Founder and Owner, CAST Enterprises LLC

Our Fulfillment Services

1. Receiving & Inventory Storage Secure receiving, quality inspection, and organized warehouse storage.
2. Pick, Pack & Ship Accurate order fulfillment with careful, protective packing.
3. Marketplace & DTC Fulfillment Support for Amazon, Walmart, eBay, and Direct-to-Consumer (DTC).
4. Repacking & Refurbishment Support Repackaging, custom FBA labeling, fnsku, and light product refurbishment.
5. Multi-Carrier Shipping FedEx, DHL, UPS, USPS and commercial freight partners.
6. Inventory & Order Management Real-time tracking, inventory updates, and order visibility.

Why Partner with CAST?

  • Strategic Southern California Location: Located in Orange County, close to major ocean ports and airports for fast distribution.
  • Fast Transloading from Major Ports: Rapid container drayage, unloading, palletizing, and transloading from Port of Los Angeles & Port of Long Beach.
  • Experienced 3PL & Logistics Team: Skilled operational staff ensuring high accuracy and zero hassle.
  • Flexible Solutions for Growing Businesses: Custom solutions scaled to your volume without high minimum commitments.

How It Works

  1. Your products are manufactured or purchased and shipped to our Southern California warehouse.
  2. We receive, inspect, and organize your inventory.
  3. Your online store or marketplace (Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Shopify) syncs orders.
  4. We pick, pack, label, and ship orders directly to your American customers.

Location Matters for Shipping Savings

Shipping from our Lake Forest, CA warehouse enables ground delivery across California, Nevada, and Arizona as fast as next day, with reduced shipping zones and costs across North America.
